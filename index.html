Happy Birthday, Rounak! 🎂

Aaj ka din tera hai. Dil se dua hai ki tera aane wala saal khushiyon, sehat, kaamyabi aur sukoon se bhara ho. Jo sapne tu apne liye dekhta hai, unke aur kareeb pahunch.

Tujhe ek bahut achha dost maana hai, aur tere liye meri wishes hamesha achhi rahengi. Is chhoti si birthday surprise ka maksad bas tere chehre par ek muskurahat lana hai.

Agar mann ho, ek chhoti si dil ki baat ↓

Kabhi kabhi main apni baat sahi tarah keh nahi paata. Meri niyat tere liye dosti aur izzat ki rahi hai; maine tujhe kabhi galat nazar se nahi dekha. Phir bhi agar meri kisi baat ya tareeke se tu uncomfortable ya hurt hua, toh uske liye dil se sorry. Teri feelings mere liye maayne rakhti hain.

Main teri space aur tere faisle ki respect karta hoon. Is wish ke badle reply ya baat karne ki koi expectation nahi hai. Kabhi tu khud baat karna chahe toh main sunne ke liye hoon. Aaj bas apna birthday khushi se mana.

Hamesha khush reh. Tere liye bahut saari duaein. ✨

— Deepak
